## Android Version (APK)
Download and install the android app directly on your phone:

- **Download APK:** Click on `onepercentgoal-v1.0.1.apk` under the Assets section below to download.
- **Installation Note:** After downloading, allow your browser/GitHub app to "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted.
